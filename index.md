## About me

I build products where money, data and people meet: who gets access to credit, how decisions get made, and how teams and workers are paid and supported.

- **Fintech and credit:** merchant finance, inventory finance, asset finance, solar finance, BNPL, lending, embedded finance.
- **Payments:** digital wallets, closed-loop payments, salary payments and inter-country salary disbursements, transfers and collections, merchant repayments and direct debit, settlement and reconciliation.
- **Data and AI/ML:** enterprise AI/data intelligence platforms, AI/ML forecasting, API-driven capabilities, product analytics.
